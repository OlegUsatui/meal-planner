# Product overview

Meal Planner helps a household choose from a shared recipe catalogue, plan meals on a calendar, maintain personal products and recipes, and see a live list of products required by upcoming planned recipes.

The first-run outcome is a planned meal: after authentication, onboarding uses the shared system catalogue to fill one date/slot without requiring personal content. The returning-user home screen is a "Today" overview with today's plan, the next meal, upcoming shopping demand, and contextual quick actions.

Primary mobile areas are Today, Plan, Recipes, Shopping, and More; Products, Settings, and account actions live under More. Desktop exposes the same capabilities in a persistent sidebar. Products remain intentionally minimal. Shopping is a read-only calculation with a seven-day default range and does not store purchase state.
