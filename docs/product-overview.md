# Product overview

Meal Planner helps a household choose from a shared recipe catalogue, plan meals on a calendar, maintain personal products and recipes, and see a live list of products required by upcoming planned recipes.

After authentication, users enter the "Today" overview directly. It shows today's plan, the next meal, upcoming shopping demand, and contextual quick actions; users can add their first meal whenever they are ready from Today or Plan.

Primary mobile areas are Today, Plan, Recipes, Shopping, and More; the «Що приготувати?» picker is available from More on mobile and has a dedicated desktop navigation item. Products, Settings, and account actions live under More. Desktop exposes the same capabilities in a persistent sidebar. Products remain intentionally minimal. Shopping is a seven-day-default calculation with local device-only purchase marks; it does not store purchase state on the server.
